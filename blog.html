Two hours of debugging, and it was one line

A timer on a client dashboard kept running an hour late — but only for users on the night shift. Same code, same inputs, and yet once the clock passed 11pm everything quietly drifted.

I blamed caching first (no). Then the queue worker (also no). Then Laravel's scheduler (closer, but still no). I added logs, replayed the flow by hand, and finally rebuilt the exact sequence in a test with a user in the Asia/Kolkata timezone.

It was timezone math — it's always timezone math. The app stored timestamps in UTC but rendered the daily window in local time, and the subtraction was applied after the offset. So the slice silently shifted by an hour, and only ever surfaced on days where the timer crossed midnight.

Three things I check first before I touch a time bug again:

  1. Store UTC, display local, and never convert twice in the same flow.
  2. Are my "same inputs" actually the same? Midnight boundaries hide differences for most of the day.
  3. Can I reproduce it outside production? A one-line test with a specific timezone beat two hours of staring.

— written so future me doesn't lose the same two hours.
