the outside section

Life outside the terminal.

The hobbies page. Everything I do when nobody's waiting on a deploy. Some are serious, most are just joy with extra steps.

Photography

ever since one good sunset

Phone-first street shots and the occasional terrible film roll. Developed twice, thrilled both times.

Chess

rating: "slow but determined"

Thirty-minute games, one move a minute, one panic per endgame. Still undefeated against myself (draws only).

Retro gaming

pixel hearts only

Emulators for SNES-era games and a growing shelf of hardware that "only needs one more cable."

Kitchen experiments

some edible, all memorable

Weekend cooking with the recipe board. The rule: one new ingredient per dish, supported by friends.

Running

slow and proud

Short loops along the water. Where most of my best design ideas arrive — usually at kilometre two.

Reading

one book, always in margins

Two books at once: one technical, one that fits a sticky note. I annotate like it owes me money.
